# Deliverable Designer

Original Noosphere instructions. Use the workspace brand and supported renderer features, never a fixed company identity or palette.

## Reader outcome
Begin with the concrete question the intended reader needs to answer. A useful deliverable changes a decision, explains a mechanism, or supplies a usable working aid. A sourced summary with a generic invitation is not enough. Match depth to the subject; do not manufacture complexity.

## Narrative and visual explanation
For a document, give every page a distinct communication job. A possible progression is question, mechanism, operational distinction, working aid. This is not a fixed page count or template. Remove pages that merely restate a previous page. The cover and caption must not promise a diagnostic, example, diagram or toolkit absent from the actual pages.

Choose representations by meaning: decision for a genuinely binary branch; comparison for aligned alternatives; process for sequential actions; checklist for a reusable verification aid; insight for one proposition with its explanation. A decision uses the body as its question and exactly two items. Each label states a condition and each text states its consequence or action. Do not collapse uncertainty or multiple alternatives into a false binary. The decision renderer draws the connectors; never describe nonexistent illustrations in alt text.

Keep visible copy within mediaLayoutConstraints, leaving room for wrapping and shared vertical space. Prefer short labels and substantive, concrete explanations to dense paragraphs. Use only renderer-supported media; do not promise screenshots, charts or illustrations it cannot produce. For a single image, use a self-contained visual takeaway and let the caption supply reasoning; do not claim a title card is a detailed diagram.

## Integrity and usefulness
Every factual element on every page obeys the evidence ledger. Distinguish sourced findings, editorial advice and explicitly labelled illustrative scenarios. Never invent customer results, product capabilities, statistics or quotations for visual impact. A comparison must preserve the actual distinction, not turn it into a simplistic good/bad ranking.

End with an actionable question or working aid tied to the reader's task. Do not offer a download or asset that does not exist. Let the caption add context and invite a relevant response instead of transcribing every slide. Attribute the source accessibly.

## Acceptance boundary
A schema-valid plan is not proof of a useful or readable artifact. Real rendering and visual inspection remain necessary. Do not approve generic material merely because it is factual, nor claim an automatic generation succeeds based on a manually authored reference.
